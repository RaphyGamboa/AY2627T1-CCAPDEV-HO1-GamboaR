About Me!

Who am I?

Hello! I'm Raphael R. Gamboa. I'm an ID 122 student taking BSCS-NIS in DLSU Manila. I made this webpage to demonstrate basic HTML tags and to share one of my favorite bands, Dance Gavin Dance. I have a few hobbies including cardistry, playing guitar, gundam, games, and watching shows.

Why Dance Gavin Dance

I grew up listening to a plethora of music regardless of genre. I enjoy what I enjoy. There's no particular reason I chose Dance Gavin Dance apart from the fact I really enjoy their music. It could be something to do with my fascination for guitars and a love for fun and busy melodies, but I think it's more of how the same band could have so many different sounds yet have a solid identity as artists. I'm particularly huge fan of Jonny Craig's vocals and the guitar parts Will Swan creates.
